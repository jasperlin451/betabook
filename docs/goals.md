# Journal goals

Goal management is owner-only. Goal reads, progress and contribution lists use the same current SQL visibility predicate as journal entries, including profile privacy and accepted friendships. APIs authenticate members before reading and return private/no-store responses. Goal activity never bypasses journal privacy or exposes notes.

Supported goals: distinct sends (optionally at a grade), first send at a new grade, training entries, distinct climbing dates, and first logged visits to direct climb areas. An area's ancestors do not count as additional visits. Undated sends and repeats do not contribute to send volume; an undated existing send at a grade means that grade is not new. All qualifying climb/area names can be expanded; training entries are not listed.

Dates are civil dates, using the timezone saved with the goal. Preset windows include existing logs in that week/month/year. Custom windows have explicit start and end dates for seasonal goals, including seasons that have already started. Existing logs in that range count. Weeks begin Monday, and months are calendar months. Weekly/monthly training goals retain one active slot; met periods appear in Completed immediately; missed results appear only after the period ends. The recurring routine stays Active with its next reset date. The current unfinished period never counts as missed. Past period targets are preserved when editing a recurring goal; progress still derives from logs, so corrected/deleted logs correct progress and completion. Editing a recurring goal updates its current and future periods. Removing a goal removes its history, never journal entries.

Creation and editing check the five-active-goal limit in the write statement, including concurrent requests. Completed one-time goals free capacity. Edits that do not increase the active count remain allowed at capacity. If a past log correction makes a completed goal incomplete again, it reappears under Active; all reactivated goals remain visible and additional creation is blocked until fewer than five remain active. This avoids hiding or deleting a user's existing goal to enforce a display cap.

Active goals sort by deadline ascending. Completed goals sort by the date achieved, newest first and load five at a time. Goals are collapsible, with an owner-scoped local preference. The title, chevron and Set goal action stay outside the collapsible surface; the title uses the same typography as Journal stats. Goal rows share equal vertical padding and reserve the same date icon space with or without a completion checkmark. The empty state uses the same highlighted panel, with “No goals set yet.” and the Set goal action; tabs stay hidden. On mobile, stats are collapsed below goals; on desktop, stats stay in the sidebar.

The existing Journal tutorial remains focused on logging, filters and sharing; those lessons are unchanged and accurate. No tutorial mutation or sample data is connected to goal persistence. The reusable form and panel have component stories, and Patterns/Goals composes those real components with deterministic local data. Outdated standalone prototypes have been removed.

Recurring history loads whole calendar months: weekly goals start with the latest three months and add three older months per click; monthly goals start with twelve months and add twelve older months per click. History continues across years, while the year selector filters the Completed list. Completed groups each recurring goal once per selected year. The summary counts distinct goals achieved: each recurring routine counts once after at least one successful period, while each newly met period can celebrate independently. Individual period results remain in each routine’s history. The year selector is hidden when the history has only one year. Recurring periods belong to the year in which they start, matching their week/month labels. Changing or deleting logs recalculates both historical results and the summary.

Goal dates use abbreviated month names, hide the current year, and show both endpoints for custom ranges (both years when crossing years). Monthly recurrence uses a full month name. The editor retains full dates. Longer row labels wrap below the goal name.

Volume goals offer `= Exactly` or `≥ Or harder` for a selected grade. The latter counts original sends at that grade or higher within the chosen discipline and window. Repeats do not count again; a send can contribute independently to multiple matching goals. Existing goals default to exact matching via migration 0041. Grade milestones keep their existing exact-grade rule.

Weekly history is grouped into one row per month, using the week start date. Numbered circles identify the first through fifth week starting in that month; a checkmark badge means met, outlined means missed. Hover, focus or tap reveals the exact week and count. Loading older periods merges them into existing month rows without renumbering weeks.

Active shows the current period’s progress, including a full bar when met, with the next reset date aligned right on the progress row. All weekly circles and monthly history are collapsed behind See history in Completed.

Monthly history uses the same checkmark circles, numbered 1–12 and grouped by year. Weekly rows contain every Monday-starting week in the month. Future slots use faint dashed outlines and are labeled upcoming, the current slot is in progress, and missing/unloaded results are neutral rather than marked missed.

Implementation: Active evaluates current recurring periods only; history queries are scoped to one goal and the requested calendar-month bounds. History cursors retain their original anchor month across calendar rollover. The initial journal load shares its goal query between both tabs. All reads continue to enforce journal visibility in SQL; no cross-request result cache is used.
